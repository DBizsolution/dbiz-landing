## Design Context

### Users
Enterprise C-suite decision-makers and technical leads evaluating DBiz as an AI transformation partner. They arrive skeptical, time-poor, and comparing against Big 4 consultancies. The algorithm pages must pass both the "boardroom impression" test and the "architect squint" test.

### Brand Personality
Precise. Authoritative. Engineered.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark blueprint / engineering-drawing aesthetic (V20). Navy-deep backgrounds, orange accent for energy and CTAs, DM Sans for authority, DM Mono for technical credibility. Instrument Serif italic reserved for one emotional punch per section. Every element should feel machined — no decoration without purpose.

**References**: Rolls-Royce technical schematics, NASA mission control dashboards, Porsche engineering documentation.
**Anti-references**: SaaS gradient soup, startup "playful" illustrations, cluttered dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. **Engineered precision** — Every line, gap, and animation exists for a reason. If it doesn't communicate, remove it.
2. **Confident authority** — The system speaks for itself. No marketing fluff, no exclamation points, no gratuitous motion.
3. **Awe through restraint** — The "wow" comes from meticulous craft, not from adding more. Fewer elements, higher fidelity.
4. **Dual legibility** — Must read as impressive at a glance (executive) AND hold up under scrutiny (architect).
5. **Brand-token discipline** — Never hard-code a hex or font family. Everything flows from CSS variables.

### Quality Bar
Flagship. These are hero-level showpiece pages. Every pixel matters.
